Research Experience

Federated Learning under Client Availability & DriftYale University · 2024 – Present
Advisor: Dionysis Kalogerias
  • Proved the first convergence guarantee for Agnostic FedAvg under random, non-uniform client availability, at rate O(1/√T) without knowledge of the participation distribution (IEEE CAMSAP 2025, Best Student Paper Finalist).
  • Designed FedAVOT, an aggregation rule that casts federated averaging as masked optimal transport between availability and importance distributions, solved by Sinkhorn scaling; proved convergence and showed large gains in low-availability regimes (ICASSP 2026).
  • Developed FedeRage, a provably convergent agnostic FL method under general client drift; extended the framework to risk-averse FL with CVaR and distributionally robust objectives.
  • Built PyTorch and cvxpy simulation pipelines for heterogeneous, non-IID federated environments.
Constrained Learning, Duality & Sampling DynamicsYale University · 2024 – Present
  • Showed when Lagrangian (primal–dual) training is justified for constrained learning (fairness, safety, risk constraints) beyond convexity, via zero duality gap for universally learnable concept classes.
  • Sole-authored a weighted SDE that implements the Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao gradient flow, coupling optimal transport and information geometry for faster sampling (Yale AI Symposium lightning talk).
Reasoning in Large Language ModelsYale University
With Amin Karbasi and Zhuoran Yang
